# World brief: Blue Team

Blue Team is a fictional land force of a fictional country, the Republic of
Aldmark. It exists to prove that Spark moves to a second, very different
organisation by configuration alone: the generator code that builds Corvane
builds Blue Team unchanged. No real force, unit, person, place, vehicle or
supplier is depicted, and nothing here describes weapons, tactics or real
capabilities. The data is administrative: people and qualifications, stores,
vehicle and equipment maintenance, garrison contracts, plans and reports.

The definition lives in `backend/spark/sim/orgs/blue_team/`
(`org.yaml`, `problems.yaml`). Labels come from the defence pack,
`backend/spark/packs/defence.yaml`.

## At a glance
